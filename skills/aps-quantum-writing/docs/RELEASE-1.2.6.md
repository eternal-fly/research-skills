# APS Quantum Writing 1.2.6

This English release updates the Writing branch and all seven journal profiles. An introduction can state the contribution once the reader has the necessary concepts, or carry a precise question into the first observation in Results. A roadmap can locate a derivation, a control, or a benchmark and explain what each contributes. [Closing the introduction](../references/branch-writing.md#closing-the-introduction)

A conclusion can open with the work when a construction is the advance, with a result that already has a clear physical meaning, or with a criterion that determines the verdict. Give the direct evidence and then the control or operating range that changes its interpretation. A proof, a simulation, and a measurement each support a specific claim. [Opening the conclusion](../references/branch-writing.md#opening-the-conclusion)

The journal guidance draws on 21 papers selected from the existing 700-paper index, three per journal. Thirteen readings used the APS version of record and eight used a fixed author manuscript. Twenty papers contain an identifiable closing synthesis. One Letter ends in Methods. Each entry gives the journal record, reading version, and passage location. Four separate, synthetic editing cases exercised the new guidance.
