APS Quantum Writing 1.2.0 integrates physical-comparison-first figure planning
and makes the full manuscript's question, key idea, proof/evidence and physical
answer explicit. Appendices and Supplemental Material use the same direct
scientific prose, with necessary assumptions, uncertainty and negative results
preserved.

- Includes fixed figure-plan fields, 21 conditional quantum-task routes and a
  structural validator, with a reproduced Windows UTF-8 test-read fix.
- Executes three paired full-manuscript cases: six synthetic main texts with
  six Supplements, plus five short supporting-prose cases in each condition.
  Raw outputs and qualitative review are included; no causal or statistical
  improvement claim is made.
- Generates and inspects one analytic figure in PDF/PNG at 85 x 76 mm.
- Runs the 27-method skill suite plus two source-consistency builder regressions
  and checks extracted release archives.
- Keeps supplied literature reviews, the 2026-10-20 five-example REVTeX audit,
  and older validation results separately dated; those are not new executions.

Install `aps-quantum-writing-1.2.0.zip`; the source ZIP contains the repository.
Verify assets with the included SHA256SUMS. See `docs/RELEASE-1.2.0.md` for the
complete scope and limits. License terms and original examples are unchanged.
