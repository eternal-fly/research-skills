APS Quantum Writing 1.2.6 adds guidance on sentence order at the end of an introduction and the start of a conclusion across seven Physical Review journals.

- Choose between stating the contribution and carrying a precise question into Results. Use the roadmap to locate the evidence that answers it.
- Lead the conclusion with a construction, a physical result, or the criterion that makes the verdict meaningful. Give the direct evidence, then the control or operating range that changes its interpretation.
- Consult the journal-specific guidance and the reading-version and passage locators for 21 papers. The review includes one Letter whose main text ends in Methods.
