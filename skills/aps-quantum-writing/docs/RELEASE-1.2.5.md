# APS Quantum Writing 1.2.5

The two data-palette collections share one lookup interface. The original collection includes the five-color cycle and the native Matplotlib `cividis` and `RdBu_r` scales. The additional collection has ten named options. The plotting style retains its established default. The ten original schematic families and Floquet preset are also available. [Visual forms and palettes](../references/data-figures.md) · [Schematic colors](../references/schematic-palettes.md)

The five-page [gallery](../assets/data-figures/gallery.pdf) uses synthetic coordinates, samples and weights. It illustrates marks, intervals, distributions, cells, fields and connections. On the final page, each row renders the same scalar array twice with identical coordinates and limits. All text in the gallery is English. [Generator](../scripts/draw_data_figures.py)

The data-palette catalog contains visual roles, colors and scale definitions. The earlier scientific-writing studies are cited at an immutable commit where their multilingual fixtures and outputs can be read verbatim.
