APS Quantum Writing 1.2.1 adds the requested narrative punctuation and literature wording to main text and supporting material.

- Use connected sentences without prose colons or explanatory dashes while preserving heading joins, mathematical notation and citation syntax.
- Describe existing research and verified authors' recent work through their scientific contributions; keep identifiers and accurate publication metadata in the bibliography.
- Apply the same conventions to local edits, appendices and Supplemental Material without deleting assumptions, uncertainty or genuine negative results.
- Include five paired synthetic editing cases with raw outputs and an explicit comparison scope. Earlier full-manuscript, figure, literature and REVTeX evidence remains historical.
- Include source and extracted-package checks, frozen version-specific packaging and SHA-256 manifests.

Install `aps-quantum-writing-1.2.1.zip`. The source ZIP is a repository archive. See `docs/RELEASE-1.2.1.md` for validation scope and limits. This is an editorial preference, not a new APS rule. License terms and earlier release assets are unchanged.
