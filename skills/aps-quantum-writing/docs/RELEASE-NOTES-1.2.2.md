APS Quantum Writing 1.2.2 strengthens direct scientific prose throughout the manuscript and supporting material.

- Omit "we do not claim", unresolved-extension statements and inventories of operations not performed, even when related to the paper or mentioned only once.
- Remove passive substitutes such as "not yet validated" and default future-work promises. Describe the completed work, actual evaluation, conditions and physical meaning directly.
- Apply a required final pass to abstracts, main text, captions, conclusions, appendices and Supplemental Material.
- Retain real negative results, null measurements, counterexamples, uncertainty and methodological conditions. Keep the existing punctuation, citation and short-format wording preferences.
