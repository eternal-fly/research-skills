APS Quantum Writing 1.2.5 is the English edition with two optional data-palette collections and a general visual-form gallery.

- The original five-color cycle, `cividis` and `RdBu_r` appear alongside ten additional palettes. The existing schematic families and Floquet preset retain their original colors.
- Browse marks, intervals, lines, bars, distributions, cells, layers and weighted connections independently of the palette. The English PDF compares original and additional scalar colors on identical synthetic arrays.
- The installable skill includes English instructions and documentation, role colors and a reproducible gallery. Earlier multilingual writing-study records remain accessible through revision-pinned links.

Download `aps-quantum-writing-1.2.5.zip` for installation or `research-skills-1.2.5-source.zip` for the repository source. The release includes checksums for the attached assets.
