The source archive records main-narrative and relevant-caption review. Its observation file contains **390 original snippets, 10 PRL late-reserved placeholders, and 128 snippets with truncation markers**. Snippets are analyst observations, not paper text or complete scientific reviews. This edition preserves metadata and rule-level evidence rather than the full historical note file; placeholders and truncated text are not reconstructed.

The PRL, PRA, and PRB ordering reflects identified and screened candidates; it is not certified as the exhaustive newest 100 papers on each journal's site. For PRX Quantum, the source audit reconciled 107 official records in its window, excluding four non-original-research items and three out-of-scope items. Journal and topic mixtures differ, so differences cannot be interpreted as causal effects of journal style. Older replacements belong to a related quantum-physics topic pool, not guaranteed one-to-one platform matches.

Source identity and final wording are separate questions. A DOI link or title/author match can identify the same work while the final author list or prose differs. For example, the source correction for [10.1103/qj9y-wk92](https://doi.org/10.1103/qj9y-wk92) records five authors in the reviewed arXiv version and an additional author in the publication. One PRA typeset version came from an author-hosted location; hosting does not make it an author manuscript.

## What supports the rules

The 23 legacy entries in [rules.json](../evidence/rules.json) retain five source examples each, plus counterexamples or protected boundaries where recorded. PRB groups include cautionary examples: five references do not mean five demonstrations of the same desirable sentence form. Official rules, observations, conditional editorial judgments, and configurable preferences remain distinct.

Only the listed passages were coded for a given feature. There is no 400-paper feature denominator, style prevalence estimate, acceptance-probability estimate, or causal test of writing effectiveness. Reading prose and captions is not comprehensive image inspection, proof verification, simulation reproduction, or experimental-quality certification.

PRL's 90/10 evidence-role split was made late. The ten reserved papers were not cited as detailed induction evidence, but the synthesis writer had already read them. This is not an independent unseen set, blind test, or preregistered validation. PRA, PRB, and PRX Quantum analyses were retrospective.

The inherited independent AI spot check covered 300 source fingerprints across PRB, PRX Quantum, and PRL; PRX Quantum identity metadata; 62 PRB and 75 PRL pinned arXiv versions; 98 available PRA metadata items; and 23 deliberately chosen passages across the four journals. This was a metadata and passage spot check, not a second independent full reading, human expert review, or scientific replication.

The public edition contains no downloaded paper PDFs, full abstracts, JATS bodies, or source-paper artwork. Public access to a paper does not itself establish permission to redistribute its text or figures.
