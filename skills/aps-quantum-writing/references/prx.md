# Physical Review X: carry an important new insight through multiple layers of evidence

## Planning and writing

- Establish the paper's center with one concrete question and one evidence-supported answer. Explain how the insight advances physical understanding, connects fields, or changes research capabilities. Technical complexity and the number of theorems do not themselves establish importance.
- Platforms, methods, and mechanisms may produce multiple layers of results, but each layer should resolve a real question about the central claim or supply irreplaceable complementary evidence. Apply the removal and restructuring tests in [Conceptual unity](conceptual-unity.md); narrative framing must not invent technical connections that do not exist.
- Organize proofs in the order in which a reader understands the problem and searches for a solution: the key obstacle, the intuition behind the solution, necessary definitions, decisive steps, and conclusion. Separately check assumptions, error measures, theorem interfaces, and circular dependencies.
- For a mechanism paper, identify what observations, processing, fitting, and inference each support, and choose controls that distinguish competing explanations. For a performance paper, compare baselines, conditions, key resources, and errors together. State necessary conditions beside the supported conclusions, and omit unrelated lists of work not performed.
- Make the introduction, discussion, and conclusion intelligible to readers in neighboring areas of physics. Write the technical abstract and Popular Summary separately. For the latter, follow the journal-specific maximum of 150 words and organize around the problem, finding, and significance, avoiding a buildup of formulas and specialist abbreviations.

## Figures

Use spatial placement to express scientific correspondence: put components beside their diagnostic quantities, give mathematical branches their actual decision criteria, and place theoretical predictions beside the results that test them. In multilayer mechanisms or computational workflows, retain object transformations, fixed versus updated states, and real connections. Schematics and data panels can coexist when they explain the same conclusion.

Otherwise follow the general [FIGURES branch](branch-figures.md), including readability at the final column width. Compact y-axis-side spacing, centered bottom labels, and approximately square ordinary data panels are configurable working defaults. Dense layouts in examples are not journal-wide requirements.
