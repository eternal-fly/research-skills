# PRA: explain the technical relationships fully

## Abstract focus

For abstract drafting or revision, read [Abstract narrative](abstract-narrative.md): route by journal, article type, and contribution; connect the supported physical answer to its evidence and meaning; check both sentence continuity and the whole argument. Follow the matching profile there; the present file retains its other manuscript guidance.

For headings, consult [Heading microtypography](microtypography.md) using `journal=PRA + article_type + heading_role`. Regular Articles use a displayed, numbered body hierarchy; a Letter's principal body headings use italic run-in `Heading.`. Choose heading names and their presence to serve the argument. For Letter DAS/appendices, distinguish the official requirements from observed production style.

A Regular Article permits a clear development of the model, definitions, construction, and tests. Show the reader which relationship in a derivation produces the physical result and what an example actually tests. Independent proofs for different code families or models can proceed in parallel: explain their shared intuition together while retaining the necessary steps in each proof.

Compress a Letter around one result that can be explained completely. Its recommended abstract sequence is article-type guidance, not a template for every PRA manuscript. See [APS formatting](aps-format.md) for specific requirements.

These are literature observations and editorial choices associated with PRA-C01/C02. For the source and version basis, see [Provenance](../docs/PROVENANCE.md).
