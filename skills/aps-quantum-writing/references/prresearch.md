# Physical Review Research: develop a verifiable physical advance around a clear question

For Letter DAS/appendices, follow the guide's distinct official-requirement and published-appearance routes.

Load this reference as needed once Physical Review Research (PRR/PRResearch) is the chosen target. It is a journal reference for planning, writing, and figures, not an additional top-level branch. Handle Regular Articles and Letters separately under [APS formatting](aps-format.md). Letters have a 4,500 APS-equivalent-word limit and cannot use PRL's End Matter arrangement.

## Planning and writing

- Organize results around an important, concrete question, and explain what the advance changes in understanding, computational capability, or experimental capability. Judge journal fit by the actual contribution within the field; do not add a PRX-like broad-impact threshold.
- Give each result a job in the argument: establish a phenomenon, identify a mechanism, test an explanation, or provide an independent and complementary constraint. Mechanism claims require corresponding interventions, ablations, or theoretical support. Let the scientific task determine the number of figures and sections.
- Define the resource accounting and baseline conventions before comparing performance. When preparation, training, sampling, postselection, or data-access costs affect the claim, include them in the same comparison as performance. Address genuine data gaps in working feedback.
- Begin a proof with the problem and key idea, then introduce the required constructions and steps. Check technical dependencies separately. Define conditions, idealized models, noisy predictions, and measured quantities beside the relevant results, using positive, concrete language to state the scope supported by the evidence.

## Figures

Keep controls on matching coordinates, normalizations, and panel correspondences where possible. When data processing affects the interpretation, make the raw quantity, intermediate estimate, and final quantity traceable. A workflow can show transformations through successive snapshots of the same circuit, graph, state, or data object, retaining real branches, constraints, and return paths. An extra layer of generic process boxes is unnecessary.

Continue to apply the general [FIGURES branch](branch-figures.md): make scientific structure recognizable and information compact. Cropping uninformative whitespace on the y-axis side, centering bottom labels on their reference spans, and using approximately square ordinary data panels are configurable working preferences. The comparison task determines the actual layout.
