# Let the Physics Determine the Narrative

Choose the relevant parts when drafting or restructuring. The goal is to help readers understand where a result comes from and why it matters, rather than to imitate a journal's voice.

## Find the question this paper actually answers

Identify the change, contradiction, impossibility, structure, or capability in the materials that most deserves explanation. Ask: if readers remember only one result, what should it change in their understanding? Use the answer to decide how much background to retain, which evidence appears first, and which calculations serve only as support.

The introduction brings readers to this question; the results answer it step by step; the discussion explains what the answer changes. These are not three repetitions of the contribution, and they need not share a sentence pattern.

## Choose the approach according to the research

- **An established mechanism:** Begin with a discriminating phenomenon, then introduce the key degrees of freedom or competing processes. Use controls or limits to explain why they produce the result.
- **An experiment:** Move from the physical question addressed by the measurement to the observable. Use controls, uncertainties, and the model to help readers interpret the signal. Introduce apparatus details when understanding requires them.
- **Theory or a theorem:** Make the object and difficulty understandable before giving the structural result and its intuition. Explain the proof along the path of understanding and discovery: goal, obstacle, key idea, necessary lemmas, and details. Check technical dependencies separately, and use examples to show how the result works.
- **A method or algorithm:** Clarify the task and bottleneck first, then introduce the key construction. Performance comparisons should establish exactly what it improves.
- **Unresolved mechanisms or a null result:** Organize reliable observations, excluded explanations, and genuinely open scientific questions into a substantive conclusion. Do not invent a mechanism merely to complete a story.

These are different entry points, not fixed section orders. Complex definitions may precede the question, a short experiment may open with an anomalous signal, and a long theory paper may begin with an overview of results. Use only what helps the current manuscript.

## Let each paragraph complete an inference

State the paragraph's physical judgment early. Let subsequent sentences show the relationship, calculation, or measurement supporting it, and end with the understanding gained. Continue in the next paragraph from a natural unresolved question rather than substituting “next” or “furthermore” for a connection.

Before an equation, explain what it captures; afterward, identify the term or limit that explains the phenomenon. Before a figure, explain what needs to be distinguished; afterward, identify the trend that makes the distinction. Curves, parameters, and steps do not automatically constitute an argument.

The following are original illustrations of sentence-to-sentence reasoning, not experimental facts or universal templates:

“The narrow peak originates from interference between the two excitation paths. Detuning one path removes the cancellation and broadens the response. The resonance is therefore controlled by their relative phase, rather than by a change in the bare linewidth.”

“Orthogonality is sufficient but not necessary for this reconstruction. The construction requires only an invertible measurement map. This weaker condition enlarges the admissible basis sets while leaving the number of independent data unchanged.”

Use these relationships only when the materials actually supply the corresponding causal control or mathematical result. The examples matter because each sentence advances the reasoning, not because of their particular vocabulary.

## Judgments that support natural expression

Let physical objects be grammatical subjects and verbs express relationships: a mode hybridizes, a bound follows, a measurement resolves. Develop the background sufficiently to explain the problem, prior work, and gap. Complete those functions before compressing repeated wording. For a full introduction or conclusion rewrite, follow the [WRITING branch](branch-writing.md). State directly the fact behind “we would like to emphasize.” If the fact is already established locally, move on to its explanation.

Develop a condition fully when it helps explain the mechanism. If it merely defines an already established model, natural references back to that model are sufficient afterward. State the supported domain, approximations, and conditions directly. Omit unperformed work unrelated to the present claim rather than appending authorial self-defense or a formulaic limitations ending. Express scientific uncertainty according to the actual evidence; do not invent a definite mechanism.

## Journal-specific reader expectations

Let article type and argument guide the choice. [PRL](prl.md) emphasizes making the central result quickly understandable to broad physics readers. [PRA](pra.md) allows technical relationships to be developed fully. [PRB](prb.md) often requires explicit connections among model, observables, and mechanism. [PRX Quantum](prx-quantum.md) needs a clear entry point across quantum-information subfields. [Physical Review Applied](prapplied.md) connects application constraints to their physical basis. [Physical Review X](prx.md) carries an important new understanding through multiple layers of evidence and explains it to adjacent fields. [Physical Review Research](prresearch.md) organizes verifiable physical progress around the specific question. These are grounds for conditional choices, not mutually exclusive syntax or fixed sections.
