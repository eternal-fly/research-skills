## PRL / PRX abstract evidence ledger

Checked: 2026-10-21. All abstract analyses below are paraphrases from complete APS published-version HTML Abstract sections, not full-paper reviews. The first six are new comparison samples; the last two were traced to the inherited corpus. Small, quantum-heavy qualitative sample: patterns are editorial inferences, not journal mandates or representative statistics.

### Official guidance

- PRL author guidance: https://journals.aps.org/prl/authors . Broad physics readership; concise summary of important new results; impact, innovation, and interest. This supports explaining the change in physical understanding or capability, not merely listing techniques. It does not mandate a fixed sentence sequence.
- PRX scope and criteria: https://journals.aps.org/prx/about . Multiple alternative criteria include discovery, major advances, new connections, new directions, paradigms, and community tools. Flexible length serves both broad readers and specialists. Do not turn “paradigm shift” into a universal requirement.
- Current APS Style Guide, February 2026, E. Abstract, printed p. 11: https://res.cloudinary.com/apsphysics/image/upload/APS_Journals_Style_Guide_Authors022326_guzwhx.pdf . Concise subjects/conclusions/results; experimental quantities, objects and conditions; self-contained single paragraph; explain acronyms; no displayed equations or tables; no numbered references (inline source details allowed). PRC structured abstracts are encouraged as an exception. The abstract guidance was inspected directly.
- **Unresolved length inconsistency:** current February 2026 guide still gives general approximate 5%-of-article and under-500-word guidance, with PRL at no more than 600 characters. Multiple published PRL abstracts read here exceed that. Do not silently substitute words for characters or treat examples as permission to ignore rules. Keep a dated source/conflict flag and verify journal-specific submission instructions/editor guidance before enforcing a hard validator. The November 2024 guide and older PRL contributors page also contained this character limit; they are not the current source to cite.

### Qualified synthesis

PRL often benefits from a decisive physical answer with subordinate supporting results; PRX can develop a framework, capability or boundary through several connected results. Neither distinction is exclusive. Valid relations include evidence, explanation, contrast, qualification, definition, operational consequence and scope, not only causality. Keep methods that establish credibility, explain the mechanism, or define the contribution. Avoid generic importance openings, research-process inventories, unqualified performance claims, unsupported impact adjectives, and compulsory final “opens avenues” sentences.


## PRA / PRX Quantum abstract evidence ledger

Checked: 2026-10-21. All analyses below paraphrase the actual Abstract section of the APS publication pages, not search snippets or Popular Summaries. This is a purposive eight-paper sample, not a statistical characterization of either journal. Official requirements and sample-derived recommendations are distinguished.

### Official sources

- PRA scope and criteria: https://journals.aps.org/pra/about . Significant, authoritative contributions in specific AMO/quantum-science areas. Inference: explain the particular physical advance and its scope without manufacturing cross-disciplinary impact.
- PRA author instructions: https://journals.aps.org/pra/authors . Technical AMO/quantum-science readership, unfamiliar terminology avoidance, and contextualized results. Current guide explicitly links the Letter abstract structure below.
- PRA Letter editorial, published 2023-01-04: https://journals.aps.org/pra/edannounce/10.1103/PhysRevA.107.010001 . Explicit abstract structure: problem in one or two sentences; main finding in one sentence; work performed in two or three sentences; wider context or outlook in one sentence. This applies to PRA Letters, not all regular articles or all APS journals. Main finding precedes procedural detail.
- PRX Quantum scope and criteria: https://journals.aps.org/prxquantum/about . Selective criteria encompass exceptional advances, connections, capabilities, or insights across multidisciplinary quantum science and technology. Inference: identify the substantive change in understanding or capability, not just a platform-specific metric improvement.
- PRX Quantum author instructions: https://journals.aps.org/prxquantum/authors . Broad quantum-information-science-and-technology audience. The approximately 150-word, nontechnical problem/advance/implication guidance is for the separately required Popular Summary, NOT an abstract word limit. Do not transfer it to the technical abstract.

### PRA actual abstract samples

#### 1. Open-system approach to nonequilibrium quantum thermodynamics at arbitrary coupling

Alessandra Colla and Heinz-Peter Breuer. Phys. Rev. A 105, 052216 (2022). Published journal Abstract.
https://journals.aps.org/pra/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevA.105.052216

Four-sentence function chain: propose thermodynamics beyond perturbation theory; identify an exact time-local master equation and minimal-dissipation principle; explain the resulting unique coherent/dissipative decomposition; use that decomposition to define thermodynamic quantities and laws and relate second-law violations to non-Markovianity.

Lesson: method-led is legitimate when the method establishes the physical meaning and uniqueness of the quantities being studied. The connecting principle is necessary, not disposable jargon. A generic background opening is not mandatory.

#### 2. Irreversibility at zero temperature from the perspective of the environment

Jader P. Santos et al. Phys. Rev. A 97, 050101(R) (2018). Published journal Abstract; historical Rapid Communication, not a post-2023 Letter sample.
https://journals.aps.org/pra/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevA.97.050101

Function chain: reframe entropy production from the environment's viewpoint; derive two contributions through a dilation approach; interpret them as correlation generation and environment-state change; relate them to irreversible currents and the Markovian limit; indicate implications for non-Markovian dynamics.

Lesson: a mathematical decomposition should be translated into physical processes. Do not claim this older sample follows the later Letter structure.

#### 3. Dissipative phase transitions and passive error correction

Yu-Jie Liu and Simon Lieu. Phys. Rev. A 109, 022422 (2024). Published journal Abstract.
https://journals.aps.org/pra/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevA.109.022422

Function chain: delimit passive protection to local Lindblad spin models without syndrome measurements; associate protection with phases and robust steady-state degeneracy; introduce Ising and toric-code examples; contrast classical and quantum degeneracies; test perturbations violating detailed balance; interpret the persistence in terms of symmetry breaking versus intrinsic topological order.

Lesson: examples are organized by a physical comparison, not listed as independent accomplishments. Preserve the qualified nature of the proposed classification rather than upgrading it to a universal theorem.

#### 4. Oscillation-free unconventional photon blockade from quadrature driving of a Kerr dimer

H. Ohadi. Phys. Rev. A 114, 033736, published 2026-09-28. Published journal Abstract; inherited-corpus source reread directly.
https://journals.aps.org/pra/abstract/10.1103/c614-6qmg

Four-sentence function chain: give the Kerr-dimer driving configuration and photon-blockade effect; identify minimum coupling Jmin = gamma/4 in the weak-nonlinearity regime U much smaller than gamma and relate this to accessible photonic molecules; connect strong antibunching and a nonoscillatory second-order correlator to detector resolvability; combine continuous/pulsed operation and drive-based disorder compensation with avoiding postfabrication cavity trimming.

Lesson: technical results immediately carry physical or implementation meaning: weak nonlinearity means accessibility, correlator shape means measurable behavior, drive retuning means a fabrication advantage. No generic importance preamble is needed. The verb demonstrate alone does not establish experimental realization. Strong antibunching is not by itself a deterministic high-efficiency single-photon source.

### PRX Quantum actual abstract samples

#### 5. One Hundred Second Bit-Flip Time in a Two-Photon Dissipative Oscillator

C. Berdou et al. PRX Quantum 4, 020350 (2023). Published journal Abstract.
https://journals.aps.org/prxquantum/abstract/10.1103/PRXQuantum.4.020350

Function chain: classical bistability provides robustness through loss; ordinary loss compromises quantum encoding; two-photon dissipation offers a different possibility; previous devices saturate at millisecond bit-flip times; redesign the superconducting oscillator; achieve over 100 seconds around 40 photons; explicitly retain the inability to prepare coherent superpositions; conclude that macroscopic bit-flip times are accessible at mesoscopic photon numbers.

Lesson: compare a quantitative result with the same-metric bottleneck and retain the scope-limiting caveat. Long bit-flip time does not prove coherence protection, complete quantum error correction, or scalable fault tolerance.

#### 6. Critical Schrödinger Cat Qubit

Luca Gravina, Fabrizio Minganti, and Vincenzo Savona. PRX Quantum 4, 020337 (2023). Published journal Abstract.
https://journals.aps.org/prxquantum/abstract/10.1103/PRXQuantum.4.020337

Function chain: establish bosonic encoding and cat-manifold confinement; contrast dissipative and Kerr mechanisms and their usual resonant setting; propose the detuned hybrid code; assess performance spectrally; identify favorable parameters; explain the nonlinear competition and first-order dissipative transition; connect strongest protection to metastable-state initialization and a preparation protocol; relate detuning tolerance to multiqubit frequency fluctuations.

Lesson: mechanism, initialization conditions, and operating constraints support the performance claim. Do not remove metastability and retain only a generic phase-transition benefit. This comparatively long, technical abstract disproves an assumed universal five-sentence or 150-word template.

#### 7. Quantum Simulation of Dissipative Collective Effects on Noisy Quantum Computers

Marco Cattaneo et al. PRX Quantum 4, 010324 (2023). Published journal Abstract.
https://journals.aps.org/prxquantum/abstract/10.1103/PRXQuantum.4.010324

Function chain: broad relevance of collective dissipation; introduce a hardware quantum simulation; derive error bounds and connect them to resources; implement two-qubit superradiance/subradiance; characterize gates through process tomography; connect noise metrics to simulation accuracy; compare noise modeling; end with favorable error scaling but substantial remaining distance from fault-tolerance thresholds.

Lesson: connect formal bounds to resources and diagnostics to simulation reliability. Counterexample: the published abstract is fairly procedural and uses a sequence of additive work items. Coherent causal storytelling is our improvement heuristic, not a universal description of every published APS abstract.

#### 8. Free Cumulants in Full Eigenstate Thermalization from Boundary Scrambling

Felix Fritzsch, Gabriel O. Alves, Michael A. Rampp, and Pieter W. Claeys. PRX Quantum 7, 043003, published 2026-10-05. Published journal Abstract; inherited-corpus source reread directly.
https://journals.aps.org/prxquantum/abstract/10.1103/6n9v-b3b7

Six-sentence function chain: motivate higher-order OTOCs as probes of information dynamics and scrambling; introduce a model whose full higher-order OTOC dynamics is analytically accessible; connect that dynamics to free cumulants in extended ETH; derive time/frequency-resolved predictions with stability away from the solvable point; explain solvability through a higher-order Markovian influence matrix; conclude with emergent random-matrix behavior from structured Floquet dynamics and a connection between free probability and solvable models.

Lesson: the final significance follows from the technical chain, rather than being a generic impact claim. PRX Quantum relevance can be conceptual connection and analytical capability, not hardware performance. Do not substitute the separate Popular Summary on the same page for the Abstract.

### Proposed behavioral acceptance tests

1. Theorem-only input: provide a mathematical theorem about a quantum channel, with no supported experimental platform or microscopic mechanism. Pass if the draft explains the theorem's supported operational/conceptual consequence and assumptions; fail if it invents an experiment, a microscopic mechanism, or universality beyond the theorem.
2. Ambiguous demonstration: provide the Kerr-dimer abstract and only theoretical/simulation evidence. Pass if the draft uses theoretical demonstration/proposal language without asserting fabrication or measurement; fail if demonstrate is silently treated as experimentally observed.
3. Bit-flip versus coherence: provide the Berdou result and explicit inability to prepare coherent superpositions. Pass if the 100-second bit-flip achievement and coherence limitation both survive; fail if the draft claims a 100-second coherent logical qubit, full QEC, or established fault tolerance.
4. Legitimate method-first paper: provide Colla/Breuer-style material where a minimal-dissipation principle establishes a unique thermodynamic decomposition. Pass if the method remains causally central and is tied to physical quantities; fail if a blanket no-method-first rule deletes the key contribution or adds empty background.
5. PRA Letter ordering: request a PRA Letter abstract. Pass if the explicit official problem/main finding/work/context ordering and sentence guidance are followed, with factual connections; fail if the main result is deferred until after all methods or if that ordering is imposed on every regular article as journal law.
6. Abstract versus Popular Summary: request a PRX Quantum technical abstract from the free-cumulant paper. Pass if necessary technical terms and the analytical-to-physical chain remain, without applying the approximately 150-word Popular Summary guidance as a mandatory abstract cap; fail if abstract and summary are confused or technical content is removed merely to imitate popular prose.


## Applied, Research, and PRB abstract evidence

Checked: 2026-10-21. This is a targeted qualitative sample of 13 unique abstracts, not a representative statistical survey. All descriptions below are paraphrases. Publication does not make every stylistic choice exemplary. Abstract reading is not full-paper scientific validation.

### Official scope and requirements

- Physical Review Applied: https://journals.aps.org/prapplied/about . Scope crosses physics and application fields; acceptance includes fresh insight into applications-based physical phenomena. This supports interpreting a method or performance gain through an application-relevant physical obstacle, not requiring every paper to contain a newly discovered microscopic mechanism.
- Physical Review Applied historical contributor guide: https://journals.aps.org/prapplied/authors/information-contributors-physical-review-applied . Page explicitly dated June 2014; says self-contained abstract, conclusions and results of general interest, approximately 5% of article and below 500 words. Do not encode the dated page alone as a current submission requirement. The current /authors URL timed out twice in this check.
- Physical Review Research scope: https://journals.aps.org/prresearch/about . Explicitly includes fundamental/applied, theoretical/experimental, and technical/methodological advances. A new microscopic mechanism or cross-field breakthrough is not a stated universal requirement.
- Physical Review Research author guide: https://journals.aps.org/prresearch/authors . General readership and avoidance of unnecessary unfamiliar terminology are official writing guidance; appropriate context is required. Neither implies a mandatory generic background sentence or fixed sentence sequence.
- Physical Review B scope: https://journals.aps.org/prb/about . Condensed matter, materials, and related areas; substantive, high-quality contribution to its field. No requirement that every abstract name an experimental material or report a new phase.

### Actionable profiles inferred from this sample

Applied: connect an application-relevant physical obstacle to a mechanism, identifiable feature or design principle, then to supported capability. Explain metrics through their objects, regimes and relevant baselines. Gates/readout compatibility can establish usability. Theory, characterization and diagnosis are legitimate; neither experiments nor performance records are universally necessary. Endings may state a capability, a conditional implementation route, or an explicit trade-off. Do not append an unsupported list of computing, communication and sensing applications.

Research: identify what becomes understood or accessible: mechanism, regimes, relation, identifiability, simulation class or error control. In mechanism work, organize findings around competition and control parameters; in method work, explain the structural reason and physical applicability. Direct question-first openings and diagnostic or boundary endings are legitimate. New microscopic mechanism, experimental demonstration and broad-field revolution are not universal requirements.

PRB: connect the material/model, physical degrees of freedom and conditions to a response, excitation, correlation, phase distinction or dynamical regime. Explain competing influences where established. Method work can instead connect computational access to many-body quantities or states. Preserve approximation and finite-size/temperature/symmetry conditions that change inference. Do not equate a modeled phase with a demonstrated material phase. A material-specific opening, mechanism discovery and application ending are not compulsory.

### Shared editing tests

- Recover one central physical question and supported answer.
- Identify the relation between neighboring claims: explanation, evidence, contrast, condition, consequence, or complementary support. Do not invent causation merely for smoothness.
- Translate faster/more accurate/more robust into a real observable, state, task, resource or capability.
- Distinguish theorem, prediction, numerical test, hardware observation and proposed extension.
- Keep truth-changing restrictions close to claims.
- Allow necessary enumeration, direct openings and non-promotional endings.
- Treat published examples as evidence of possibilities, not perfect templates or predictors of acceptance.
