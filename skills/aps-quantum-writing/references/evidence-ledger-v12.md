# Three-journal expansion evidence ledger

The source expansion dated October 9, 2026 added Physical Review Applied, Physical Review X, and Physical Review Research: **100 main-body and main-caption review records each**. The English edition inherits this evidence; it does not claim 300 additional readings during translation or 700 newly read papers. The [combined index](../evidence/papers.json) contains 700 unique DOIs, with the [legacy 400](evidence-ledger.md) kept distinct.

## Sampling and identity

- Applied: 96 recent records matched Crossref and arXiv journal references, supplemented by three older readable replacements and one manually verified source. Ninety-nine manuscripts have an arXiv-metadata DOI relationship; the remaining work was matched by title, authors, and APS record. A pinned author version can differ from the publisher's wording.
- PRX: accessible official original research was screened for quantum and related physics; title, DOI, and article type were checked against official JATS records.
- Research: candidates within the date window were screened for topic and body availability. Access gaps and 14 unread reserve candidates were recorded separately; those reserves are not counted in the 100.
- These are accessible, screened convenience samples, not random samples, journal-wide representative samples, or certified exhaustive latest-100 lists. Topic and article-type differences can affect observations.

This edition summarizes the original review observations at rule level and does not republish all 300 historical per-paper notes. Detailed appendices, supplements, bibliography accuracy, and proof correctness were outside comprehensive review scope. Mathematical extraction can lose layout; prose review is not independent proof checking or experimental reproduction. The evidence supports conditional editing choices, not causal claims about writing effectiveness or journal-wide style statistics.

Distributed evidence contains public metadata, links, source fingerprints, original observations, and scope summaries. It excludes full paper text, complete abstracts, JATS content, downloaded manuscripts, and original figure artwork. Available access is not blanket redistribution permission.
