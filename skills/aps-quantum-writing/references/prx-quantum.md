# PRX Quantum: provide a shared entry point for different quantum-information audiences

For headings, consult [Heading microtypography](microtypography.md) using `journal=PRX Quantum + article_type=Research Article + heading_role`. Use a displayed, numbered body hierarchy, and determine appendix levels separately. Headings may be called Outlook, use content-specific names, or be omitted to suit the argument. Do not apply PRL/Letter run-in formatting.

Make the task, key idea, and resulting capability easy to understand before developing the technical machinery. A long theoretical article may include a results overview, a simple example, or a map of theorems. A short experimental article may move directly from the physical problem addressed by the apparatus to the measurements. Resource comparisons need a clear task and accounting units so readers can judge their significance.

Write the Popular Summary separately for nonspecialist readers, explaining the problem, advance, and use. Replacing a few words in the technical abstract does not produce a popular summary. See [APS formatting](aps-format.md) for specific requirements.

These are literature observations and editorial choices associated with PRXQ-R01/R02/R03/R06/R09. For the source and version basis, see [Provenance](../docs/PROVENANCE.md).
