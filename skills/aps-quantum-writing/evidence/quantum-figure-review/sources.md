## PRL-001

Criticality on Rényi Defects at ( 2 + 1 ) D O(3) Quantum Critical Points — DOI 10.1103/7zzj-78fv. [Reviewed version](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2605.00104v2); [journal record](https://journals.aps.org/prl/abstract/10.1103/7zzj-78fv).

Version: Pinned author arXiv manuscript. Review: targeted_local_inference. Extent: main results/discussion pp2-5 and captions Figs1-4; introduction not reread

Locations: Figs1-4, pp2-4

- Cut geometry defines defect observables. Correlation plus Binder cumulant discriminate disordered and ordered regimes; power law with correction term is used for decaying correlations, polynomial-in-1/L extrapolation for putative finite intercepts. Near-critical n=4 correlator and squared magnetization intercept discrepancy exposes remaining finite-size corrections; n=10 agreement is stronger. Size-cutoff stability and another lattice model provide distinct controls.
- Scope/counterexample: Authors acknowledge apparent order could be finite-size manifestation of very small scaling dimension. Do not convert Binder crossings or finite intercept fits into unconditional thermodynamic proof; sampled Renyi indices and finite L must remain visible.
Pixel inspection: PDF p4 Figs3-4. Single-column rows/columns align observable versus Renyi condition; separate Binder versus n panels reuse size colors. Compact axis labels approach crowding.
