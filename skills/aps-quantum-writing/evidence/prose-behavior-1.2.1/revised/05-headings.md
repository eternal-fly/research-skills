\prlrunin{Noise mechanism}
A common field acts identically on the two basis states and therefore contributes only a global phase. A differential field separates their energies, causing their relative phase to change with time. Under independent Markovian dephasing, the populations remain constant while the off-diagonal element decays at rate $4\epsilon$.

\prlrunin{Note added.}
Recent work by Rao and Sun \cite{rao2026} provides a counterexample showing that removing irreducibility can destroy uniqueness of the stationary state.
