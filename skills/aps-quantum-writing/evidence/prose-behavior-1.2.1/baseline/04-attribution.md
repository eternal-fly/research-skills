The 2016 preprint arXiv:1601.00000 \cite{archive2016} conjectures that a size-independent spectral gap survives weak perturbations. The 2022 journal article \cite{bound2022} proves the narrower result that the gap is positive for each finite irreducible chain. Positivity at each finite size does not establish a uniform lower bound, so the conjecture remains unproved.
