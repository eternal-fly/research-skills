\prlrunin{Noise mechanism} A common field acts identically on the two basis states and therefore contributes only a global phase. By contrast, a differential field separates their energies, causing the relative phase to change with time. Independent Markovian dephasing leaves the populations constant while the off-diagonal element decays at rate $4\epsilon$.

\prlrunin{Note added.} The online preprint by Rao and Sun, arXiv:2610.00002 \cite{rao2026}, provides a counterexample showing that removing irreducibility can destroy uniqueness of the stationary state.
